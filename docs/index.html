Your Privacy Comes First
We do not sell your personal data. We will never ask for real bank, Demat, PAN, or Aadhaar details. GrowPlay is a simulation-only platform.

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 · GrowPlay v1.0.0

1. What Information We Collect
  • Account Data: Email address and chosen username upon registration.
  • Simulator Profile: Trading avatar, streak records, and XP points.
  • Paper Trading Activity: Simulated orders, mock holdings, quiz completions.
  • Feedback & Support: Messages submitted via Help & Support (category, text, timestamp).
  • Device Info: OS version and device type for crash diagnosis only.
2. How We Use Your Data
  • To maintain and sync your virtual portfolio across sessions.
  • To compute weekly Arena league standings and display your avatar on the leaderboard.
  • To track educational milestones and award XP for lesson completions.
  • To provide and improve app features (bug fixes, performance improvements).
  • To respond to your feedback and support requests.
3. Data We Do NOT Collect
  • Real bank account or financial institution credentials
  • Demat or broker account information
  • Government ID, PAN, Aadhaar, or passport details
  • Payment card information (no in-app purchases exist)
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Microphone, camera, or contacts access
4. Storage & Data Security

All user data is stored via Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) policies. All communication is secured with TLS/SSL encryption. Local device storage (AsyncStorage) is used only for instant UI caching and is encrypted at the OS level.

5. Third-Party Integrations
  • Yahoo Finance API: Retrieves NSE/BSE stock quotes. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Supabase Auth: Token-based encrypted session management. GDPR and SOC 2 compliant.
  • Twelve Data API: Optional market data fallback. No user PII is shared.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion
  • Update your username and avatar anytime (in-app).
  • Clear local cache and reset virtual portfolio (Account Settings).
  • Download a copy of your data by contacting support.
  • Request permanent account & data deletion at any time.

To delete your data, email upthrivetechnologies@gmail.com with subject "Data Deletion Request". We process within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

GrowPlay is intended for users aged 13 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us immediately if you believe a child has provided their data and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notification. Continued use of GrowPlay after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
